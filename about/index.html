About Me

I'm Daniel White, owner of White Piano Services. I provide piano tuning, cleaning, regulation, voicing, and repair services throughout Chester County and the surrounding areas.

Alongside serving my own customers, I also provide tuning services for two local piano retailers, including Cunningham Piano Company, where I work as a piano technician. These opportunities allow me to work with a wide variety of instruments and continue developing my skills.

I believe in providing honest recommendations and helping piano owners understand their instrument's needs. I take pride in careful workmanship and attention to detail, approaching each job thoughtfully. Whether it's a routine tuning or a more involved repair, my goal is to provide reliable service and an experience you can feel confident about.

My favorite part of this work is getting to meet the people behind each piano. I enjoy hearing what an instrument means to a family, whether it's been passed down through generations or is where a child is just beginning to learn. As a father of three little ones, I especially enjoy meeting the curious kids in the homes I visit. I'm always happy to let them take a closer look and be part of the process, hopefully sparking the same appreciation for these instruments that I have. I take pride in combining my knowledge of pianos with the care and attention each family deserves.

Honest Advice. Thoughtful Repairs.

This is the player piano I grew up learning to play on. It was far from perfect, with worn keys and plenty of character, but it was still a meaningful part of my childhood. That experience has shaped how I approach piano care. I believe in being honest about a piano's condition and helping owners understand their options. Sometimes a repair is worthwhile; other times, the cost of the work may outweigh the value of the instrument. My goal isn't to recommend work simply because it can be done. It's to help you make an informed decision about what makes sense for your piano, your needs, and your budget.
