GET TO KNOW YOUR PIANO TECHNICIAN

About Me

I'm Daniel White, owner of White Piano Services. I provide piano tuning, cleaning, regulation, voicing, and repair services throughout Chester County and the surrounding areas.

Alongside serving my own customers, I also provide tuning services for two local piano retailers. One of these is Cunningham Piano Company, where I also work as a piano technician in their warehouse. These opportunities allow me to work with a wide variety of instruments and continue developing my skills.

I believe in providing honest recommendations and helping piano owners understand their instrument's needs. I take pride in careful workmanship and attention to detail, approaching each job thoughtfully. Whether it's a routine tuning or a more involved repair, my goal is to provide reliable service and an experience you can feel confident about.

BEHIND THE WORK

The People Behind the Pianos

My favorite part of this work is getting to meet the people behind each piano. I enjoy hearing what an instrument means to a family, whether it's been passed down through generations or is where a child is just beginning to learn.

As a father of three little ones, I especially enjoy meeting the curious kids in the homes I visit. I'm always happy to let them take a closer look and be part of the process, hopefully sparking the same appreciation for these instruments that I have.

I take pride in combining my knowledge of pianos with the care and attention each family deserves.

MY APPROACH TO PIANO CARE

Honest Advice. Thoughtful Repairs.

This is the piano I grew up learning to play on. It's where I fell in love with playing, and it was deeply cherished despite being far from perfect. That experience has shaped how I approach my work today.

I treat every piano with care and attention, and enjoy helping owners understand their instrument's condition, the issues it may have, and the options available. There is often more than one way to address a problem, and I take each person's individual needs into account when discussing possible solutions.

My goal isn't to recommend work simply because it can be done, but to help you make informed decisions that make sense for your piano and your budget.
