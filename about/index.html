About Me

Hi, I'm Daniel White, owner of White Piano Services.

I've loved pianos for as long as I can remember. Whether I'm playing, tuning, or repairing one, I truly enjoy every part of the process. There's something incredibly rewarding about helping a piano sound and feel its best so it can continue bringing music into someone's home.

My favorite part of this work is getting to meet the people behind each piano. I love hearing what an instrument means to a family, whether it's been passed down through generations or it's where a child is taking their first lessons. It's meaningful getting to be a small part of those stories. If there are curious kids around, I'm always happy to include them in the process, hopefully sparking the same appreciation for these instruments that I have.

My goal is simple: provide honest, reliable service while treating every piano with the same care and attention I'd want for my own. Whether I'm there for the occasional tuning or regular maintenance, I hope you come away with a piano that sounds better, and an experience that leaves you feeling confident your instrument is in good hands.

Thank you for considering White Piano Services. I look forward to meeting you and your piano.
