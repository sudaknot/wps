GET TO KNOW YOUR PIANO TECHNICIAN

About Me

I'm Daniel White, owner of White Piano Services. I provide piano tuning, cleaning, regulation, voicing, and repair services throughout Chester County and the surrounding areas.

Alongside serving my own customers, I also provide tuning services for two local piano retailers. One of these is Cunningham Piano Company, where I also work as a piano technician in their warehouse. These opportunities allow me to work with a wide variety of instruments and continue developing my skills.

I believe in providing honest recommendations and helping piano owners understand their instrument's needs. I take pride in careful workmanship and attention to detail, approaching each job thoughtfully. Whether it's a routine tuning or a more involved repair, my goal is to provide reliable service and an experience you can feel confident about.

More Than Just PIanos

The People Behind the Pianos

My love of piano is what first brought me into this work, but it's the people I've met along the way that have made it especially meaningful. I really enjoy getting to know the families I visit, and I often leave my appointments smiling.

As a father of three little ones, I especially enjoy meeting the curious kids who want to see how everything works. I'm always happy to let them take a closer look and be part of the process.

It is incredibly rewarding knowing that my work can make a positive difference in someone's experience with their piano. Whether it's helping an instrument feel and sound its best or simply giving a family a better understanding of its care, I want every visit to leave people feeling good about their piano and the care it receives.

MY APPROACH TO PIANO CARE

Honest Advice. Thoughtful Repairs.

This is the piano I grew up learning to play on, and the instrument that fostered my love of music. Although it held great sentimental value to me, its condition left much to be desired, and the cost of restoring it would have far outweighed the practical benefit.

That experience has given me an appreciation for the personal significance pianos can hold for their owners. I approach every instrument with care and attention, taking the time to explain its condition, discuss possible solutions, and consider each owner's individual needs.

My goal is to provide honest, thoughtful guidance—not to recommend work simply because it can be done, but to help you determine what is worthwhile for your piano and your budget.
