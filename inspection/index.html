What Does an Inspection Include?

I'll take a look at the piano's major structural and mechanical components, including:

  • Soundboard and bridges
  • Pinblock and tuning pins
  • Plate and strings
  • Action and keyboard
  • Cabinet and general condition

I'll identify visible concerns, note any areas that may need attention, and provide recommendations where appropriate.

A Lesson I Learned Firsthand

Years ago, I acquired a piano without really knowing what I was getting into. I knew it needed some work, but I didn't realize it had a broken bridge and ultimately wasn't even tunable.

It taught me how important it is to understand an instrument's condition before investing time and money into it.

Sometimes a piano is worth restoring, and sometimes the most helpful advice is knowing when not to invest further. I want to provide that same honest perspective with every inspection.
