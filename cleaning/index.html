WHAT'S INCLUDED

Attention to the Details Inside Your Piano

A piano has many areas where dust and debris can accumulate, some of which are not visible during everyday use. A cleaning focuses on these interior spaces as well as the accessible exterior surfaces.

Soundboard

Dust and debris are removed from the exposed soundboard and surrounding areas, helping keep the interior of the piano clean and presentable.

Tuning Pins & Plate

The exposed tuning pins and plate area are cleaned to remove accumulated dust and debris.

Action & Action Cavity

The action and the cavity beneath it are cleaned to address dust and debris that can collect around the piano's moving parts.

Case & Exterior

Accessible exterior surfaces and interior case areas receive attention to help improve the overall appearance of the instrument.

BEHIND THE KEYS

Cleaning Beyond the Visible Surfaces

Much of the dust inside a piano settles in areas that are hidden from view. The action cavity, in particular, can collect a surprising amount of debris over time.

Cleaning these areas requires access to the piano's interior and careful attention around its components.
